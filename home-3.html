How It's Made

Four steps to obsessive quality.

01
🐄
Select the Beef
Only A5-grade Wagyu passes our sourcing criteria. We personally visit every partner farm twice a year.
02
🧂
72-Hour Marinade
Whole cuts submerge in our house-made marinade for a minimum of 72 hours. Every fiber absorbs flavor completely.
03
🌬️
Low & Slow Dry
Dried at 165°F for 8–10 hours. Preserves the Wagyu fat profile and creates the perfect chew that no shortcut can replicate.
04
📦
Hand-Packed Fresh
Every bag is hand-packed within 24 hours of production and ships with a freshness guarantee. No distribution centers.
